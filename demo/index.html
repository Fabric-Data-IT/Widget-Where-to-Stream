Where to Stream

Show your readers where a movie or series is available in their country — subscription, free, rent or buy — with one script tag and one HTML element. Style-isolated with Shadow DOM, about 5 KB gzipped, no dependencies.

Sample data. This demo runs against a mock of the Origin Nexus API: the /v1/where-to-stream endpoint is not live yet. Titles, prices and logos are placeholders.
